# Debrief – VerifAI User Study

Thank you for taking part in this study.

This research evaluates the usability and perceived trustworthiness of an AI-based deepfake detection system. The goal is to understand how real users interpret AI outputs and whether explanations help improve confidence and understanding.

Deepfake detection is an active research area, and current systems are not perfectly accurate. The outputs shown during this study should not be treated as definitive or authoritative.

If you have any concerns or questions about the study or the topic of deepfakes, you are encouraged to seek further information from reliable sources.

Thank you again for your contribution.
