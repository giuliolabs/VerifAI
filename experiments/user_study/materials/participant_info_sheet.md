# Participant Information Sheet

## Study Title
Usability and Trust Evaluation of an AI-Based Deepfake Detection System

## Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in a study evaluating the usability and trustworthiness
of an AI system designed to detect deepfake videos.

## What You Will Do
- Interact with the VerifAI system
- Upload or test videos for deepfake detection
- Complete a short post-task questionnaire (approx. 5 minutes)

## Risks and Benefits
There are no known risks associated with participation.
Your feedback will contribute to improving AI transparency and usability.

## Data Handling
- No personal identifying data is collected
- Responses are anonymized
- Data is used strictly for academic purposes

## Voluntary Participation
Participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw at any time.

## Contact
For questions, please contact the project researcher.
